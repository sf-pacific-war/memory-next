FROM KEYWORDS TO ORIGINAL SOURCES

How to use WWII Pacific War

  1. Enter a clue

    Search for titles, places or people. Preset topics expand to Chinese, English and Japanese terms, shown above the results.

  2. Narrow your search

    Combine sources, material types, catalogue dates and access filters. Date filters use catalogue metadata, not inferred battle dates.

  3. Check the original source

    Open a record to read its original description, then visit the archive to verify its content, access and reuse conditions.

Search covers imported local samples, not live searches of entire collections. A keyword match does not establish historical relevance, and a catalogue record does not guarantee online full text.
