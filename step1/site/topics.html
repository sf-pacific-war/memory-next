How to use WWII Pacific War

  1. Enter a clue

    Search for titles, places or people. Preset topics expand to Chinese, English and Japanese terms, shown above the results.

  2. Narrow your search

    Combine sources, material types, catalogue dates and access filters. Date filters use catalogue metadata, not inferred battle dates.
